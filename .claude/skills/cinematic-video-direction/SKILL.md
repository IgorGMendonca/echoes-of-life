# Cinematic direction
Inspect project README, package scripts, assets and installed tools first. Reuse existing architecture; use Remotion official skills when present. Use FFmpeg for inspection, cutting and validation when available. Never claim video generation, asset download or render success unless verified. Do not invent missing footage; report missing inputs and use an explicit preview placeholder only if requested or necessary.

## Visual language
- 1080x1920, 9:16, 30 fps by default; H.264/AAC MP4, yuv420p. Adapt to existing project specs if necessary.
- Blue hour city lights, passing cars, rainy windows, train rides, sunsets, empty classrooms, familiar streets, quiet ordinary moments. Prefer real moving licensed or owned footage. Generated footage is acceptable when allowed and correctly identified.
- Subtle grain, restrained color grading, believable motion and gentle transitions. Use shot variety and intentional framing, not random clips or perpetual zooms on stills.
- Aesthetic edits: 7–15 seconds, 1–3 shots, simple text or no text, no narration by default. A seamless edit is fine for aesthetic pieces, not a claim of Creator Rewards eligibility.
- Narrated pieces: 65–90 seconds, shots motivated by the story; initial target 2–5 seconds per shot but let pacing breathe. Use a narrative arc and visual progression instead of a repeated template.
- Keep important text away from platform UI; inspect rendered frames using a safe-area overlay if possible. High contrast, legible typography, at most two caption lines, natural line breaks; avoid rapid flashing.

## Audio and assets
Recommend 2–3 tracks or musical directions per video; identify title and artist when known, but do not claim rights or availability without checking. Do not download or embed unlicensed commercial songs. If music will be added in TikTok, export a music-free version and specify suggested cue points; keep voice and music separate when practical. For embedded audio use only verified permitted assets; track provenance and license requirements, attribution, and any AI disclosure.

## Deliverables
Timed storyboard (start/end, shot, movement, text, sound); asset manifest with source/license status; actual MP4 if tools and assets allow; caption and optional SRT; music suggestions and a music-free master where relevant. Use unique filenames and avoid overwriting originals.
