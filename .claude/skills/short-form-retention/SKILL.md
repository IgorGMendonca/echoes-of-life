# Retention and editorial experiments
Optimize for a satisfying viewer experience, not emotional manipulation. Never fabricate metrics or promise virality.

For each video: create three honest hooks (specific observation, concrete scene, relatable question). Choose one that matches the actual payoff. Remove preambles, filler, fake suspense, repetitive text and false urgency. Make the first frame understandable with sound off. For longer videos introduce meaningful changes of visual or narrative beat without arbitrary cuts. End with a complete thought.

## Batch strategy
Default: 2–3 short aesthetic edits (7–15s), 1 emotional story (65–90s), 1 human psychology video (65–90s) when production capacity allows. Vary theme, mood and visual setting. Separate growth content from potentially eligible long-form content; duration alone does not guarantee monetization. Do not reuse identical clips or scripts to inflate volume. No platform publication without explicit authorization.

## Learning loop
Maintain a local content log of ID, format, topic, hook, duration, music, publish date, views, average watch time, completion, shares, saves and follows when available. Use actual data only. Compare videos of similar format and age at consistent checkpoints (24h, 72h, 7 days). Record sample size and avoid conclusions from tiny samples. Test one meaningful creative variable at a time. Keep a backlog of hypotheses, not guaranteed algorithm tricks.
